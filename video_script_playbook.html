💡 THE 30-SECOND MENTAL MODEL (How to Understand & Explain This Project Easily)

If anyone asks you what this project does in plain English, use this fruit market analogy:

"Imagine you visit a market where one vendor sells 10 kg boxes of 95% pure gold quoted per 10 grams, while another vendor sells 1 gram packets of 99% pure gold quoted per gram. If you just look at the screen prices, you are comparing apples to oranges!

Gold Intelligence does 3 simple things:
1. Purity Normalization: Converts every single contract into ₹ per 1.000 gram of 100% pure gold.
2. Anomaly Detection: Watches the relative spread and flags when one contract becomes abnormally expensive or cheap compared to historical norms.
3. The Reality Check: Rejects fake 'paper profits' that would get wiped out by Indian exchange taxes (CTT, GST, Stamp Duty) or illiquid market depth."

5. Tough Judge Q&A Cheat Sheet (Defense Playbook)

Q1: "Why relative value instead of predicting directional gold price?"
Your Answer: "Directional speculation is exposed to macro events, interest rate decisions, and geopolitical shocks. Relative-value is market-neutral. Because contracts represent the same physical commodity, their normalized prices must converge by expiry. By trading the basis spread, our beta to gold is 0.002 — we don't care whether gold crashes or rallies."
Q2: "Isn't this just a Bollinger Band on a price spread?"
Your Answer: "No, applying a raw Bollinger Band on MCX gold spreads is suicidal for two reasons: First, un-normalized spreads are distorted by differing purities and quotation units. Second, over 75% of 2-sigma events are false traps occurring in illiquid hours or failing Indian statutory fees. Our innovation is the deterministic purity transform combined with the Triple-Filter reality hurdle."
Q3: "How do you handle Indian commodity taxes and slippage?"
Your Answer: "We explicitly model the full Indian statutory fee schedule: Commodity Transaction Tax (CTT) at 0.01%, Stamp Duty at 0.002%, MCX Turnover fee at 0.0021%, GST at 18%, plus 3.5 basis points of bid-ask slippage per leg. If an anomaly's net expected reversion doesn't exceed our 14.0 bps hurdle, we discard it."
Q4: "Is there any look-ahead bias in your backtest?"
Your Answer: "Strictly zero. All rolling statistics (μ and σ) use trailing 20-day windows with no knowledge of future bars. Trades detected at bar close t are executed on bar open t+1. We also simulate automatic liquidation 5 days before contract expiry to prevent delivery notice liabilities."
